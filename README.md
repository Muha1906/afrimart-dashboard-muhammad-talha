# AfriMart Sales Dashboard
Project Overview
The AfriMart KollyBright Sales Dashboard is an Excel-based sales analytics dashboard designed to provide a clear view of business performance.
The dashboard summarizes revenue, profit, unit sales, country performance, product performance, and sales trends over time. It uses interactive filters for country and year to make the analysis easier.
🎯 Business Questions & Answers
1. What is the total revenue?
Answer: The business generated ₦830,516,300.00 in total revenue.
2. What is the total profit?
Answer: The business generated ₦170,022,600.00 in total profit.
3. How many units were sold?
Answer: A total of 83,843 units were sold.
4. Which country generated the highest revenue?
Answer: Kenya generated the highest revenue among the countries shown in the dashboard.
5. Which products have strong unit sales?
Answer: Bread Family Loaf and Palm Oil are among the higher unit-selling products shown in the dashboard.
6. How did revenue change over time?
Answer: The trend chart shows that revenue decreased from 2024 to 2025.
7. Which products are highlighted in the product analysis?
Answer: The dashboard compares products using unit sales, revenue, and a Top 5 Products chart to highlight product-level performance.
